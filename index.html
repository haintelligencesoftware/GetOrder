GetOrder: Expand Business

A daily growth coach for restaurant, café and delivery-kitchen owners. Log orders and revenue in 20 seconds, watch your trends, and follow a step-by-step roadmap to win more orders.

Download on the App Store

What you get

  • Daily check-in – orders, revenue and mood per branch or platform, with a streak to keep you consistent.
  • Analytics – weekly and monthly charts and a Growth Score that shows how you are doing.
  • Growth Roadmap – 10 phases and 81 practical tasks: menu and storefront, reviews, pricing and profit, loyalty, direct channels, operations, new customers, team and finance. Quizzes lock in what you learn.
  • Private by design – your data stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • English and Turkish.
Pricing: the app is free. The Growth Roadmap is a one-time purchase (lifetime access, no subscription).
